Woo,Sang-Wook(Legend of Bangpae fighting)

| Post views:

 

 

His Career

Born 20th,Oct,1939

He entered Bangpae fighting world in 1970s.

He has been flying fighter Bangpae more than 40 years from 1970s.

 

Why is he called legend of Bangpae?

He is in his middle of seventies,but still healthy and energetic.He had been a motive of successive challenging to all Korean Bangpae fighters to overcome him.His line cut results had been near mythology till his middle of sixties.It's not too much to say that his kite was the origin of present Bangpae in Korea as a fighter kite.

 

source : Drachen
