Hole effect

Peter, Nam 2013. 3. 22. 00:58

| Post views:

Source : The Analysis of Traditional Kite's Aerodynamic Force Characteristics along The Design Factors Change.

Written by Kang,Chi-Hang, professor of R.O.K Air Force Academy, approved to be extracted,translated and posted.

 

 

When we cut a central hole in Bangpae, the CL,CD loss are about 20% compared to no hole one, but sudden CL drop 1.2 to 0.9 over the critical angle of attack in no hole Bangpae doesn't happen. How do you think this will influence to the performance as a fighter kite? It seems that kite can show very stupid reaction near this attack angle  because sudden drop in CD,CL means sudden less line tension we can encounter and difficult to control kite head changing during line touch or cut game.

 

Test specimen is as following Bangpae with bowing depth a, width b, length c, central hole radius r.

 

 

Apparatus to measure CL(lift coefficient) and CD(drag coefficient).

 

 

No hole Bangpae shows relatively sudden CD,CL drops after stall starts near attack angle 35(deg).

 

 

 

80(mm) diameter hole Bangpae shows relatively smooth CD,CL drops after stall starts near attack angle 30(deg).

 

 

 

 

No hole Bangpae shows relatively sudden aerodynamic forces drop after stall starts in both bowing depth 9(mm),and 33(mm).
