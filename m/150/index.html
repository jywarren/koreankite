2015 Gyung-Joo tournament, final Chang-Jin Gong vs Dong-Yi Hong

Peter, Nam 2015. 3. 23. 13:19

Hong Dong-Yi is on the left side Gong Chang-Jin is apart from him about 30 meters on the right,

the very video taken person.

 

Gong's "L" turn quick winding and sudden releasing at 3:04.

I guess his kite is on his front where wind pressure strongest whereas opponent's kite is relatively at right.

At this timing he turns kite head then wind line quickly, furthermore hits the final blow with sudden line releasing(=quick sawing). Can you hear the sound of "full Twi-Gim" ?
