Science of Hanji (Korean Paper)

Peter, Nam 2013. 1. 19. 02:18

| Post views:   

 

The proverb "紙千年絹五百" means silks sustains 500 years and Korean paper lives 1,000years.We can find this from

"MugujungGwang-DaraniGyoung" Korea's the world oldest woodblock printing heritage.

 

[MugujungGwang-DaraniGyoung] <source: Korean Cultural Heritage Administration>

 

 

Hanji lives 1,000 years long


When MugujungGwang-DaraniGyoung was found in 1966, we can read its contents out despite of its damage and corrosion by

long years oxidative action. What kinds of secrets are concealed in traditional Korean paper has endured over 1,200 years

compared to current paper has lifetime less than 100 years.The words Hanji-Korean paer- handmade with the paper mulberry

skin has originated from  the late period of Chosun dynasty to distinguish it from the machine-made Western paper introduced

to Chosun by Japanese.We can define Hanji as handmade Korean paper with skins of the paper mulberry cultivated in Korean

peninsula.

 

What are Hanji made with?

Main material for Hanji is skin of the paper mulberry and subsidiary one is hibiscus root.The paper mulberry grows well in southern

part of Korean peninsula as Jeonra province and Geongsang

province where has soil of wind gneiss or granite gneiss withered.

Sometimes the others materials as paper-bush, a native mulberry, mulberry are used but the paper mulberry has been used most

commonly because of its long and strong fiber.Mucus of hibiscus

root acts as a dispersant that helps fibers to be spread and float

well on the water during making paper.Its used quantity also

influences on the water drain speed through fibers to paper

thickness and rigidity of made paper.

 

 

Traditional Hanji making process


1) Ingathering the paper mulberry → 2) Boiling → 3) Stripping of its bark → 4) Soaking barks in water → 5) Boiling →

6) Washing and bleaching → 7) Batting → 8) Spreading in water bucket→ 9) Scooping paper → 10) Squeezing water out →

11) Drying → 12) Beating → 13) Ironing.

 

1) Ingathering the paper mulberry
Most prefered one-year-old paper mulberry cutting has been done from November to Februery.Main fiber material for Hanji

had been the paper mulberry till 7 century of Korea dynasty but as paper demanding increased, materials others like hemp

,mulberry,rice straw,reed also had used from then.We can find this in books published during early Chosun dynasty times.

 The paper mulberry

Hibiscus root

 

2) Boiling
Ingathered paper mulberry must be boild then striped in a month after cut because it is taken off well while contains sufficient

moisture.

 

3) Stripping of its bark(black skin -> blue skin -> white skin)
After boiling it for 5 hours then it striped well, next  solar dry it, and soaks it in cold brook water for 10 hours, finally scrape

the black and blue skin results in white skin.Remain black and blue skin lowers in paper quality. 

Stripping of its bark

 

Scraping black skin

4) Soaking barks in water
Soak white skin in cold brook water for one or two days to soften and remove impurities in white skin, then solar dry it.

5) Boiling

Boiling white skin

Process to boil the sufficiently water soaked

and cut to 30~40 centimeters white skin with

lye for 8 hours.Lye acts to eliminate the

lignin and other impurities that can discolor

paper to yellow color.

6) Washing and bleaching
Wash boiled white skins in cold brook to remove impurities and bleaching it by solar drying.Even more white

fiber skin can be obtained through this procedure.

7) Batting

Firstly, drain water washing and bleaching completed skins for

a while on clean and flat stone, then bat them to fritter.

This process continued till the volume reaches twice of

the beginning.Quality of batting process can be checked

by spraying water on it  and see whether fiber spread well or not.

 

Batting process completed white skin

8) Spreading white skins in water bucket

Spreading in water bucket

Process to put the batting completed white skins in water

bucket and mix well.

9) Scooping paper

 

- Single rope scooping : Traditional Hanji making method and

sift frame sustained only by one rope and no barrier along edges,

it enables skin mixture flow to be free and form # shape fiber

combination.Paper made by this methods shows tougher than that

of two rope one.But it takes more time to make paper.

Korean kite fliers would use only the Hanji made by this method.

Scooping paper

- Double rope scooping : Introduced from Japan in early of 20 century and sift frame sustained by two rope and has

barrier along edges, it blocks skin mixture to flow over barriers and makes water drain through sift mesh.Paper made

by this methods shows weaker than that of one rope one because of fiber arrangement difference from that of

one rope one.But it takes less time to make paper and enables to get steady paper thickness. Korean kite fliers would

not like to use paper made by this.

Wet Hanji just after scooping

 

10)  Squeezing water out

Rolling wooden roller to squeeze water out

Spreading bamboo screen on wooden planks, and put

paper scooped  planks reversely, then press wooden

roller to dehumidify. It's very important to be cautious

scooped paper not being wrinkled stacking.

It's convenient to put a thread between each paper to

separate paper easily after paper drying.

Finally put heavy stone on stacked papers to squeeze

water out.

11) Drying
A day squeezed paper contains more than 60% of moisture content.Two kinds of drying, one is solar drying on wooden plate,

the other is steel plate that is heated by hot water steam or heat inside.

Separating dried paper

 

Solar drying

12) Beating

Process to spread and smoothen paper wrinkles by beating with

wooden roller or threadmill.This process is helpful for Hanji to get

 lint-free and prevent Hanji having excessive hygroscopic property.

It makes fiber combination in Hanji be compact and its surface

be sericeous.

Beating

 

13) Ironing

When ironing added, Hanji get better surface.

 

Act of Lye

It's mixture of water and ash from rice straw,buckwheat straw,bean stem. We call it ash water that has acidity pH 10 to 12,

alkalinity. It enables to remove impurities in fiber and to keep fiber  be decorative.Acidity of Hanji is pH 7,neutrality.

It makes Hanji lives more or less 1,000 years long.

 

The position of Hanji in the world

Korea imparted papermaking method to Japan over 1,300 years ago. The Chronicles of Japan, Nihon Shoki,

written in 720,states that paper making method was introduced to Japan by those Korean buddhist priests, Damjing and

Bubjung, in 610. When it's asked to foreigners work for museums in the world with the question "Which paper is the best

in the world?", answers from eight of ten persons might be "Washi, Japanese paper."

*It might be not correct in some meaning, on the other hand shows their ceaseless efforts throughout generations as family jobs.
