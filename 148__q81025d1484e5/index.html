4 Korean Bamboo Species for Bang'Pae Spars

Peter, Nam 2015. 3. 20. 20:11

It's told that the best for Bang'Pae is Boon-Jook, Latin name "Phyllostachys nigra (Lodd.) Munro var. henonis (Bean) Stapf)," Genetically closest(0.269) are between Oh-Jook and Boon-Jook.

 

 

Reference :  연댓살대나무4종학명1.pdf

 

Following shows Boon-Jook [Phyllostachys nigra (Lodd.) Munro var. henonis (Bean) Stapf)] is the best species in mechanic properties ] among 3 species of Korean bambooes, WangDae, Boon-Jook and Maeng-Jook.

 

 

Remarks)

1. Phyllostachys bambusoides Siebold et Zuccarini

    = Wang-Dae

2) Phyllostachys nigra (Lodd.) Munro var. henonis (Bean) Stapf

    = Boon-Jook

3) Phyllostachys pubescens Mazel

   = Maeng-Jook

 

 

 

Reference :  왕대_분죽_맹죽 물성치비교논문.pdf

 

 

Both above are different species, the left seems Wang-Dae, the other seems Boon-Jook.
