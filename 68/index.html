    of same bamboo changes a little bit better.

One more report on bamboo MOE by its age, height position, depth from its skin.

It shows 3 to 5 years old bamboo has highest MOE. 

No significant difference found by its height position except 1 year old bamboo.

Figure<Bamboo classification>

 

With bamboo getting older, SG(Specific Gravity) become bigger and MOR(Modulus of Rupture) and

MOE(Modulus of Elasticity) get increased. Correlation coefficients(R^2) that MOR is 0.91, MOE 0.71 shows

SG and bamboo property are deeply related with.One year old bamboo has big MOR and MOE differences

between top and bottom whereas almost no difference found in older than 3 years bamboo.

 

 

We can see measured SG datas as per bamboo age,height, wall depth.No big differece found in outer SG data among

3 and 5 years old bambooes that are commonly used for kite spars. SG value of top part is slightly better than bottom.

 

 

We can see the MC(moisture Contents) % data variation of three years old bamboo from the bottom(node NO.1) to

the top(node NO.31).
